# Qualitative material

* `interview_guide.md`: semi-structured guide used in the five interviews (second half of June 2026,
  20–35 min, first author, without the company co-author). Transcripts are confidential and not released.
* `codebook.csv`: the 12 inductive codes (Sec. VIII-A–C), organised under the three RQ2 areas, with an
  application criterion, participant-level presence (one count per participant and code; a participant
  may contribute to several codes), the count out of five, and one illustrative quotation per code with
  its participant id (M1–M5). Quotations are English translations of the Italian originals.
* `questionnaire.md`: the closing questionnaire (Sec. VIII-D), scale and anchors (1 = strongly disagree …
  5 = strongly agree; O1/O2 free text, coded into categories), five respondents, descriptive analysis
  only (mean, median, sample SD; Sec. VIII-D). Individual answers are not released.
  The questionnaire was administered in Italian; `questionnaire.md` reproduces the English translation
  of the items used in the paper.
