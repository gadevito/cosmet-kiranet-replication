# Semi-structured interview guide (RQ2)

Five individual interviews (M1-M5), 20-35 minutes each, second half of June 2026 (after the last Phase 2 measurements), in Italian, conducted by the first author without the team lead present;
recorded with consent and transcribed. The first two authors coded the transcripts
independently with an inductive approach, reconciled differences in a joint
session and grouped the codes into three themes (changes, benefits, frictions).
Quotations in the paper are English translations. Transcripts are confidential
and not released.

Guide (open; order adapted to the conversation):
1. Your role, years at the company, COSMIC experience before November 2025.
2. How you measured a use case before CosMet, and how you do it now.
3. Changes in responsibilities and in effort per measurement.
4. When you receive a draft: what you look at first, what you check most carefully.
5. A case where the draft was wrong: how you noticed, what you did.
6. Perceived benefits (for you, for the team).
7. Obstacles and frictions: requirements, tool, infrastructure, examples.
8. The model runs on a machine in the office: does that matter to you?
9. Advice for another company considering the same move.
